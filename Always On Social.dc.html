01

Hyundai Motor Company

Dec 2021 – Apr 2022 Content operations · Instagram, Twitter, YouTube

Feed rebranding for a global brand: high-volume content planning, ideation and production direction, delivered under frequent revisions from HQ. Final delivery — 30 images and 10 videos.

Strategy

Production calendar. Four content types — choice-based, test-drive events, informational, seasonal — scheduled to run simultaneously without quality slipping under volume.

Iterative design partnership. Fast-changing HQ feedback translated into revised direction quickly and accurately.

Global-brand rigour. DEI compliance review across every published asset.

711

02

Dhuman

Oct 2020 – Aug 2021 Project manager · pet food brand

Full ownership of content planning, client communication and production direction — building a trust-driven strategy around real customer stories rather than product features.

Strategy

Pets are family. Owners are already motivated to share that relationship — so the content had to be genuine personal storytelling, not spec sheets.

Interview series as flagship. A consumer interview video format made authenticity the brand's main asset.

Feed kept warm. Re-gramming tagged posts, event and informational content between releases.

Outcome
500K

average views per interview video

0

paid promotion — reach and trust grew organically through sharing

Execution · consumer interview series

I found customers who had posted real reviews, reached out personally, then ran the whole production — planning, scripting, filming and edit supervision.

03

Goobne Chicken

Apr 2021 – Dec 2021 Project manager · Facebook page, 9 months

End-to-end content planning and performance reporting for a chicken franchise — hands-on food styling and on-camera presenting alongside continuous data analysis.

Strategy

An engagement, not a campaign. Weekly, monthly and quarterly performance analysis fed the next month's content direction and ad spend.

Pairing and seasonality. Storylines built around food pairing and seasonal moments to hold a teen-skewing audience.

Outcome
+8.3%

page followers, ~378K → 410K in 9 months

185K

organic reach by Jan 2022, up from 146,869 in Mar 2021

Execution · styled and presented myself

I styled the food for every shoot — plating and art-directing chicken, pizza and dessert — and appeared on camera presenting pairing ideas, including creating and naming the "Chi-Pi-D" combo. Shooting plans for the regular sizzle content were developed directly with the in-house video team.
