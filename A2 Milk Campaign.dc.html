A2 Milk Launch Campaign

As Korean consumers showed growing interest in premium milk, Yonsei Milk launched its own A2 protein milk. I led an integrated digital campaign built around a tailored digital funnel, owning the full process from awareness-building to purchase conversion for the new product.

The Challenge

At launch, the domestic A2 milk market had a single player — New Origin, an imported brand — so there was an open field but no established category language. The pressure came from the other direction: Seoul Milk, Korea's No.1 dairy brand, was expected to launch its own A2 milk within five months, which turned the brief into a race for first-mover advantage. Everything had to be built to win awareness and conversion before that entry.

Product truths to build on

Three product facts carried the whole strategy. No A1 protein — easier to digest for anyone who feels discomfort after milk. The only domestic A2 milk — manufactured in Korea, so quality and freshness were provable rather than claimed. And a composition close to breast milk — A2 beta-casein closely resembles the protein found in breast milk, making it a naturally suitable source of nutrition for a child.

Strategy

In Korea, A2 milk was strongly perceived as milk for children. To pull New Origin's customers across, we shifted the frame from the product to the job it does — milk → jobs to be done → not milk, but a mother's choice — and made post-weaning mothers the main target. A second group was being missed entirely: people who avoided milk because it upset their stomach, without ever recognising why. They became the sub target, and each group got its own message, its own film and its own media route.

The media plan ran as one full funnel: search → sponsored content → landing → promotion, closed by a retargeting loop. Paid media on Netflix, YouTube, Naver and Instagram carried reach and the two films; credibility was built underneath with SEO-driven Naver blog content, YouTube branded content and native-style review posts on Naver and Instagram, so anyone searching "A2 milk" met the brand three times over. The online store converted with discount promotions and review events, and visitor retargeting returned non-converters to the top of the funnel — with real customer reviews feeding straight back into sponsored content.
