The Challenge

The brief asked for a piece of marketing communication that would persuade overseas students that UCD Smurfit offers not only a leading-edge business education but a genuine expansion of their creative potential — that studying there would let them "Think Otherwise". The real difficulty was the abstraction. The theme descends from George Berkeley's "We Irish think otherwise" and Apple's "Think Different", both philosophical rather than practical, and it had to become a concrete, authentic story an international student could recognise — without turning into a generic school-promotion video.

Concept

The idea came from one small piece of everyday English: the suffix -wise, which we use to reframe how something is being looked at — perspective-wise, career-wise. Put a blank in front of it and the competition theme fits inside your own sentence: think ( ) wise. It ties the wordplay straight back to "Think Otherwise" and gives the film an ownable structural device instead of a slogan pasted over campus footage.

The device

think ( ) wise

Fill the brackets and you get the chapter. Perspective. Approach. Career. Each one a way the school changes how a student thinks.

Creative device · the opening hook

The film opens on a text-message exchange with a friend — "Hey, you won't believe it, I've become a different person" — so the first voice you meet is personal rather than institutional. It buys attention in two seconds and hands the story to a student before any of the "-wise" chapters begin.

Narrative structure

Three "-wise" chapters, each cut as a before-and-after — a "Used to be…" moment against a "Become…" transformation — so the change is shown rather than claimed.

Chapter 01

Perspective-wise

Used to be seeing things one way, studying alone.

Become seeing through others’ eyes — group debate and collaboration.

Chapter 02

Approach-wise

Used to be linear, narrow thinking.

Become layered and reframed — mind maps, sticky notes, library resources.

Chapter 03

Career-wise

Used to be feeling lost, passively scrolling job sites.

Become actively engaging — career services, GradIreland, job fairs.

Production

As team lead I directed the shot list and storyboard, mapping each "-wise" segment to specific on-campus footage — library, classroom debates, the career centre, campus walks — then coordinated filming logistics and led the edit, assembling the segmented structure into a single coherent cut inside the two-minute limit, entirely without generative AI tools.

Shot list & storyboard

Every chapter tied to named locations before anyone picked up a camera.

Filming logistics

Scheduling cast, crew and campus access around a live teaching term.

Edit

Cutting the before-and-after pairs so the structure reads without narration.
