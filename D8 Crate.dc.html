At-Home Date Night Box

A digital retailing project in which I helped build D8 Crate — a fictional at-home date night subscription box — from concept through to a live website with tracked performance analytics. I led social media for two months, planning content calendars, producing posts and generating AI product imagery, while contributing to the website build and using GA4 to measure and optimise performance.

01

The Challenge

Ireland has become the second most expensive country in Europe, and a mid-range dinner for two now averages €90 — which quietly prices the ordinary date night out of reach. The gap isn't desire; couples still want the evening. What they've lost is an affordable way to have it, so the brief was to rebuild the restaurant experience at home for a fraction of the bill, then prove people would actually sign up for it.

Objective

Design and launch an affordable, culturally-themed date night box that recreates a restaurant experience at home — then validate demand and drive sign-ups through owned and paid social, with the site instrumented well enough to tell us which theme people actually wanted.

02

Solution

D8 Crate is an affordable alternative to an expensive dinner out that still feels like an occasion. Each box is a culturally-themed date night — Italian, Korean or Japanese — holding eight curated moments rather than a hamper of food: Dine, Drink, Indulge, Set the mood, Discover, Connect, Play, Remember. Everything arrives ready, so there is nothing to plan. Two tiers, Basic at €50 and Premium at €69–85, were priced on a cost-plus model and validated with conjoint analysis, putting a full evening at roughly half the €90 restaurant bill.

03

Strategy

Social was my core focus. The content calendar ran on the three cuisine themes, alternating product-introduction posts with playful engagement formats — cultural quizzes, "which K-pop do you like" prompts — and countdown or invitation-style creative as launch approached. With no physical product to shoot, AI-generated imagery produced consistent, on-brand visuals across all three crates. Instagram led with TikTok in support; Reels carried relatable, real-life dating situations in a playful tone, and paid amplified whichever organic pieces performed.

  • The target audience is already highly active on the platform.
  • Content built on romantic, date-night scenarios aligned with the brand's visual identity.

04

Website & measurement

I supported the build of the D8 Crate site and its Italian, Japanese and Korean crate landing pages, then instrumented them properly — GA4, Google Search Console, Google Tag Manager and Microsoft Clarity — so acquisition, conversion funnels and on-site behaviour were all observable rather than assumed.

A/B test · 4 landing pages

Which crate theme people actually wanted

Running the four pages against each other identified the top-performing theme and let ad budget move behind it instead of being split evenly on a guess.

Clarity · sessions & heatmaps

Diagnosing the drop-offs

Session recordings and heatmaps showed where visitors stalled, which drove fixes to both the mobile and desktop experience.
